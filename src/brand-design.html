Beck’s Process

From a design perspective, your brand’s visual identity starts with your logo! We work with you to design a single concept for your overall brand identity, which will include your main logo, two alternate logos, color palette, and typography information. We also include a website mockup for your homepage and brand guidelines with personalized recommendations.

  • You complete your questionnaire. We’ll review, ask questions, and start to develop a concept & direction for your brand.
  • We can hop on a video chat or communicate via email to solidify details before the design phase begins.
  • Design & presentation of the concept
  • You have the opportunity to ask for changes, if needed. We include two revision rounds for small changes to refine the concept.
  • Your new brand is finalized! We prepare all of the files for your use & provide those to you via email.
  • You show off your new logo!

If you require additional concepts or revision rounds, we’ll get you set up with a payment link to purchase! We always welcome back our clients who need additional design items for a full brand build-out, and value on-going relationships. Small businesses should support other small businesses!
