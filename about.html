About Rock Paper Branding

Rock Paper Branding is a US-based, veteran-owned company. We’re a husband & wife team with 20+ years of experience in the business world and 15 years working as creative professionals.

We take a lot of pride in the human side of business interactions, and we believe that the key to success for everyone is communication. We aim to be approachable, share our knowledge, and form lasting relationships with the people behind the business.

We think that knowing a bit about the people you hire is so very important - I mean, after all, you’re trusting us with your business.

It’s not our first rodeo!

You might have worked with us before! Our first brand & website design company was sold in 2023 after 9 years in business. It was time for me to focus my full energy on font design, and Jeff focused his full energy on web development and marketing.

Our previous success was a wild ride, and we learned a lot of lessons that we can apply to RPB. We never left the design world - we simply took a different path for a little while and it led us right back here!

We’ve pretty much always worked together in one way or another. We brainstorm, problem-solve, fine-tune, and implement personalized solutions for small business owners that want something more than clocking in & out of a dreadful 9-5.

Think we’re a great fit? Send us an email & let’s get to know each other!
